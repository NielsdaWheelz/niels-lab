# COMMITTED: TELL

**Epigraph:** *Every stratum was once the surface.*

## THE IDEA

The site is the excavation report of an inhabited mound. A tell is a hill made of settlements that refused to leave — each city built on the compacted ruin of the last. A man who has been five things is not five men; he is one mound.

First 60 seconds: a dark page bearing a typed section drawing — a soil profile rendered in text, the way real reports draw Harris matrices. The top stratum is labeled with today's date and is alive (/now, /log — the occupation layer). Below it, numbered strata descend: research, engineering, the novels, the poems, the iron, and at the bottom, ice — the profile bottoms out at ice, not soil. The profile *is* the navigation. You click a stratum; you get its register of finds; the finds cross-reference each other — *cf. NN-071* — and following those threads is the entire game. The site holds no state, no progress, no unlock: the only save file is the visitor's mind. Depth of experience equals depth of reading. That is the Outer Wilds thesis, kept honestly.

## HOW THE LISTS LIVE IN IT

Shonagon's lists were finds trays. The eight lists become the registers of small finds, byte-sacred and untouched — each one-line entry catalogued with a find number, and the existing zero-JS evidence-disclosure becomes the find's context card: findspot, date, associated material. The apparatus is new; the objects are not. The numbering runs by depth, so before you follow a cross-reference you can feel how far down it goes.

## ONE SIGNATURE MOMENT

The mound accretes while you watch. /log entries older than a season are formally *accessioned* — assigned find numbers, compacted into the stratum below the surface. A returning visitor finds last spring's training log has become archaeology. The living site buries itself.

## WHY ART, NOT GIMMICK

The metaphor is the meaning: nothing in a life is deleted; it compacts and bears load. Every stratum is also a destruction layer — the hockey career ends the way a burn layer does, and the report says so in the flat register of field archaeology, which is where the melancholy lives. It survives visit ten because accretion is real (the surface you stood on is now buried) and because comprehension genuinely compounds: NN-118 in the poetry stratum re-reads the scar in the ice stratum.

## THE FACETS

- **Hockey player** — the ice stratum is bedrock: everything above cross-references down into it; it references nothing below, because there is nothing below.
- **Strongman** — its register sorts by kilograms; measurement is its descriptive language.
- **Novelist** — the only stratum with conjoining fragments: sherds of one vessel found across several layers, the formal device for fiction drawn from the whole life.
- **Poet** — the smallest finds receive the longest context cards; inverse proportion as law.
- **AI researcher** — every find number resolves as plain text and JSON; the dig publishes its own dataset, and the machine reader is treated as a fellow excavator.
- **Engineer** — the colophon is the report's methods section: tooling described as sieve sizes and trench technique.

## THE RISK

Theme-park archaeology — sepia, dust textures, torn paper, Indiana Jones. The single policing rule: **nothing enters that could not appear in a real excavation monograph.** The conceit may live only in apparatus — numbering, registers, section drawings, cross-references, field prose — never in ornament.
