# THE PARISH

*Dear reader — I have watched one parish my whole life, and it has not once repeated itself.*

**THE IDEA**
The site is one continuous letter, years deep, from a naturalist to a fellow enquirer he's never met, describing the sole specimen he's had lifelong access to: a man. Not a private journal — outward correspondence, in the line of Gilbert White mailing Selborne's swifts to strangers. In the first sixty seconds you land mid-letter, salutation already spent, no hero, no welcome — as if you'd opened someone else's ongoing mail. You aren't the visitor. You're the correspondent he's been writing to for years without an answer.

**HOW THE LISTS LIVE IN IT**
The eight Shonagon lists become eight notebooks kept the way White kept one on swallows, another on weather — not surveys, ~60 dated sightings each, never reordered, only added to. /log is the same notebook continuing daily. /writing holds the fair copies made for publication. /projects is specimens still alive in captivity, experiments unconcluded. /cv is the formal catalogue filed with a society that wants facts, not prose — which is exactly why it stays dense and unlyrical, no contradiction at all. /colophon is the naturalist's note on his instruments: how each observation was taken, and with what.

**ONE SIGNATURE MOMENT**
Under every claim sits a pinned specimen label — the disclosure element the site already uses for evidence, given new work. Open it and it never just proves the sentence above; it mildly contradicts it. The letter: *"He plays with unusual violence for a man his size."* The label, dated: *"3/14 — watched him take a checking penalty for what looked, honestly, like bad temper. Not violence. Spite."* Every exhibit is the raw field note the polished sentence was written from, and it is always a shade less flattering than what got sent.

**WHY ART, NOT GIMMICK**
White spent two decades on one parish and never claimed to finish it, never dramatized himself, only recorded what recurred. Applied to a personal site, that stance rebukes the genre — no highlight reel, no arc, no résumé posing as a story: a life given the same patient, dated, occasionally unflattering attention a naturalist gives a badger sett. It survives a tenth visit because rereading is the only thing this form was built for — noticing, late, a connection between two entries the writer never remarked on.

**THE FACETS**
*Hockey player* — logged as behavior in the wild: motion, aggression, migration, never a stat line. *Strongman* — logged as specimen measurement: girth, load borne, morphology, nothing about how it felt. *Novelist* — the interpretive marginal note laid over raw observation, typographically distinct, admitting it's a guess. *Poet* — the namer: each entry earns a descriptive epithet the way a species earns a common name, never a title. *AI researcher* — the dichotomous key at the back: if X, go to 14; if not, go to 21 — classification as literary form, long before it had that name. *Engineer* — the footnoted instrument: what apparatus took this measurement, and how it was calibrated.

**THE RISK**
Failure mode: Ye Olde Nature Journal — sepia parchment, quill type, foxed textures, a skeuomorphic page-turn. The rule: nothing appears that White couldn't have made with pen, paper, and a straightedge. No texture, no illustration, no antiquing — only diction and structure carry the period: dating, address, the specimen tag. If a choice evokes costume rather than a notebook actually kept, season after season, it's cut.
