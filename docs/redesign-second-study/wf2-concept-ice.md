# PRESSURE RIDGE
*Where six currents meet, the ice cannot lie flat.*

**THE IDEA.** The site is one plan-view sheet of river ice, lit the way Turner lights fog — no gloss, no skybox, just diffuse cold light through cloudy water. It arrives flat and silent: one line of epigraph on a calm approach. Moving toward center, the sheet is already under visible compressive stress from six sides with nowhere to go but up — it lifts into a ridge: six overlapping wedges, angled like fault blocks, meeting along one jagged seam through the page's middle. That seam, not a nav bar, is the table of contents. Each wedge holds its own tilt; hovering one levels it toward you until you face its cut surface directly.

**HOW THE LISTS LIVE.** The lists are the ice, not text laid on top of it. Each wedge's cut face shows banding, like tree rings — one band per list entry, accreted in order. The zero-JS disclosure already used sitewide turns geological: a checkbox-hack reveal that looks like a thin skin thawing in place to expose the buried line and its citation. Bands returned to often carry a faint worn sheen — an honest, unboasted trace of attention.

**ONE SIGNATURE MOMENT.** Where two wedges meet at the ridge, their bands don't align. The hockey wedge's 2019 band is thick; the poetry wedge's 2019 band, from the identical calendar year, is a hairline. The seam is visibly, measurably uneven — a jagged little fault line a visitor could screenshot and caption: *this is where his years don't match up with each other.*

**WHY ART, NOT GIMMICK.** A life across six vocations isn't one integrated sheet — it's plates pushed together by the same pressures of time and ambition, refusing to fuse smoothly. The misalignment at the seam is the honest record; smoothing it flat would be the lie. It survives visit ten because the geology keeps aging: each new log entry thickens one band a sliver, nudging the silhouette, so June's fracture is subtly not September's.

**THE FACETS.**
- *Hockey player* — the approach glides with frictionless scroll-snap easing, then meets the ridge with a hard, boarded stop: open-ice speed arrested by collision.
- *Strongman* — dwelling the cursor on the seam, like a held lift, slowly reveals thin compression-vector lines from both sides — visible only under sustained effort.
- *Novelist* — disclosure text sets in a ragged, justified manuscript margin, past tense only: marginalia, never UI copy.
- *Poet* — the seam's jagged vertices are secretly set to the syllable count of one unpublished short poem — real prosody, undetectable without measuring.
- *AI researcher* — each wedge's tilt is computed client-side from a live embedding-similarity check against today's /log entry — small, real, curl-visible inference, not shader decoration.
- *Engineer* — the fracture pattern is deterministically seeded from the last deploy's git commit hash: reproducible, versioned geology, auditable in source.

**THE RISK.** The failure mode is a demo-reel ice shader — glossy parallax, crack sound effects, texture over nothing. The rule: no crack, band, tilt, or grain renders unless it traces to one inspectable value — a list entry, a date, a commit hash, a similarity score. If no one can name the data a pixel encodes, it gets deleted.
