# CORE

*"What we call a life is only pressure, held long enough to become rock."*

**THE IDEA.** Not a website — a borehole. On arrival: total dark, a thread of light at the top like a drill bit, one word beneath it — DESCEND. No header, no menu, only a vertical shaft. Scrolling drills downward through strata, each its own material, color, grain, and prose register, laid down at a specific depth. Depth runs backward through time. The surface is today — the living log, still being deposited, growing upward with each entry. The deep bands are childhood. The bottom is marked and unreached: *bedrock still forming.*

**HOW THE LISTS LIVE IN IT.** The eight Shonagon lists are core samples pulled from the depths where each facet solidified, pinned to the rock face as specimen cards — closed by default, torn open (the zero-JS disclosure) to show the primary evidence: a scan, a box score, a manuscript page. The dense CV is the driller's own ledger, a monospace depth/material/date table running the margin. /now is the live drill-bit position. /writing is a second hand's annotation beside the core. /colophon is the rig specification — the stack that dug the hole.

**ONE SIGNATURE MOMENT.** The depth gauge isn't in meters. It counts revisions of the first sentence of his unpublished novel — that sentence prints in the margin the whole way down, mutating draft by draft as you descend, until at bedrock it reaches the one clause that has never changed in nineteen years of rewrites. That is the thing a friend would repeat.

**WHY ART, NOT GIMMICK.** A life as a table of contents pretends its selves took turns. As strata they are simultaneous — hockey, iron, prose, verse, code, compressed under the same pressure into adjacent bands that touch but never resolve into one another, the actual condition of a person. Digging costs the reader something real: scroll fatigue as the honest price memory charges. It survives visit ten because the top keeps accreting while the bottom never runs dry.

**THE FACETS.**
- *Hockey player* — one band runs on a shift clock: sentences clipped to thirty seconds, then cut, whether the thought is finished or not.
- *Strongman* — one band where the scroll itself loads: added resistance on the wheel, so descending there is a lift, not a glide.
- *Novelist* — the margin ruler is his revision history; the site's unit of depth is a sentence he keeps rewriting.
- *Poet* — the grain turns to verse sideways: line breaks as horizontal seams, illegible as prose, readable only once you tilt your head.
- *AI researcher* — a fossil band: a small model's output on his archive, baked once at build time, never re-run — extracted and dead, not performing live.
- *Engineer* — the rig stays visible: a persistent panel (real depth, real build hash, real commit) as furniture, and view-source reads like an annotated boring log.

**THE RISK.** Geology-as-skin — stock rock textures, scrollytelling parallax, a mineral favicon — is the failure mode. The rule that polices it: no pixel may depict stone. Every stratum is built only from type, weight, spacing, and monospace grain. The instant an actual image of rock appears, the piece has failed.
