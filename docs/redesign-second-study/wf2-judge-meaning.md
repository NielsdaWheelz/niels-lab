## THE MISS

Every concept in the field re-siloes the man into six facets — strata, warp threads, films, wedges, hours — and then works to reconnect what it just severed. But Shonagon's list is already the anti-silo: "Things that are exact: a wrist shot, a deadlift, a line of iambic pentameter" gathers unlike domains under one *quality*. That is the zuihitsu's whole architecture, and not one concept trusted it. The miss: **a site whose deep structure is the disposition, not the vocation** — organized by the verbs and qualities the eight lists already name (the rep, the cut, the hold, the revision), where each page draws its evidence from all six lives at once and the six selves emerge only as recurring witnesses who keep testifying to the same qualities. Hockey and strongman can never be casualties in that form, because no form exists for them to be exiled *from* — they show up inside every quality, exactly as they do inside the man. The concepts built sixteen taxonomies for a writer whose chosen genre exists to dissolve taxonomy. The truest site would let the lists, not the lives, be the load-bearing walls.
