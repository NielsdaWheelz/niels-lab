The five choices, unbundled

The plates were bundles. These are the actual decisions, each independent. My pick marked on each — together they are what I would now spec: the wall, wearing the page’s margins.

ground
Paper or night. pick: night — your canon is night writing; the leaf survives as the print CV’s world, the one daylight room.
proof
In the margin, always visible — or opened, set heavy. pick: the margin — the site’s ethic made spatial; on narrow screens it folds inline, nearer, never gone.
order
Even type or settling type. pick: settling — the one mechanism that puts your spine into the typography; tuned subliminal in production, legible here so you can judge it.
clock
Static or hour-alive (epigraph and open leaf follow the real San Francisco hour, server-side). pick: alive — cheap, honest, Shōnagon’s own index.
ledger
Uniform rows or the material law: weight settles, ink dries, and the struck failure fades faster than its lesson. pick: the law — confined to /log, where recency is meaning.
