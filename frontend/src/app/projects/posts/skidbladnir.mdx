Skíðblaðnir, skid for short, is a
[Go gateway, CLI, and terminal UI](https://github.com/NielsdaWheelz/skidbladnir/tree/b0dd12a6d9865dac8938fdbba9d0fae7072d7cc0/cmd/skidbladnir)
on each computer, plus a
[Kotlin and Jetpack Compose Android app](https://github.com/NielsdaWheelz/skidbladnir/tree/b0dd12a6d9865dac8938fdbba9d0fae7072d7cc0/android).
Each computer runs
[its own gateway](https://github.com/NielsdaWheelz/skidbladnir/blob/b0dd12a6d9865dac8938fdbba9d0fae7072d7cc0/README.md#how-it-works)
beside its tmux, reached over Tailscale; no computer relays another's
terminal. I specified, built, and run it for my own three computers and one
phone.

The first decision was where sessions live. Before a scope reset to a tmux
remote, my design kept its own state: SQLite as authority, hook and control
sockets, and a registry of agents. The reset's answer:
["tmux is the database and the process supervisor."](https://github.com/NielsdaWheelz/skidbladnir/blob/b0dd12a6d9865dac8938fdbba9d0fae7072d7cc0/docs/history.md#2-tmux-as-the-store)
skid keeps its metadata in tmux user options, so a restart lists tmux again
and a session started by hand appears in the inventory. The cost is a process
per read: on 21 October, on one Mac with 18 to 20 agent sessions, the gateway
used 13.6% of a core, its children 17.5%, and the tmux server 5.0%. Since
v0.22.0 a budget bounds a steady listing to two tmux processes; I have not
remeasured.

The second was what a lost reply means. The first draft answered with
[durable operations](https://github.com/NielsdaWheelz/skidbladnir/blob/b0dd12a6d9865dac8938fdbba9d0fae7072d7cc0/docs/history.md#3-at-most-once-never-replay):
idempotency keys kept for 90 days and a recoveries endpoint. I kept its
refusal to guess and dropped the machinery. Every effect on an existing
session is one tmux command queue: a
[guard](https://github.com/NielsdaWheelz/skidbladnir/blob/b0dd12a6d9865dac8938fdbba9d0fae7072d7cc0/internal/tmux/guard.go)
on the server lifetime and session id, the effect, and an applied marker.
Without the marker the outcome is `unknown`: reported, never retried. A lost
join reply leaves the invitation spent.

The third was
[herdr](https://github.com/NielsdaWheelz/skidbladnir/blob/b0dd12a6d9865dac8938fdbba9d0fae7072d7cc0/docs/history.md#6-herdr-adopted-then-abandoned).
On 22 September I planned to hand terminals, pane processes, and agent
observations to it as a maintained upstream, and keep skid's Android app,
gateways, and CLI. v0.7.0 ran over herdr on all three hosts on 24 September.
On 25 September I split the tmux product back out so both could run side by
side, and by 30 September I had retired herdr. herdr's interface did not suit
me: it leans on the mouse and on shortcuts I found arcane, and pairing the
phone was laborious. Having used skid, minimal and keyboard-first, I would not
go back. The episode took eight days and two releases. skid's status research
borrowed herdr's separation of detection from attention, its explicit
unknown, and its startup readiness.

Numbers, from GitHub's release lists on 2026-11-20: 80 releases between
2026-08-27 and 2026-11-20, 39 of them in the first repository, now
[herdr-mobile](https://github.com/NielsdaWheelz/herdr-mobile/releases), and
41 in
[its current one](https://github.com/NielsdaWheelz/skidbladnir/releases).
Over tracked non-binary files, my
[reduction campaign](https://github.com/NielsdaWheelz/skidbladnir/blob/b0dd12a6d9865dac8938fdbba9d0fae7072d7cc0/docs/history.md#9-the-48-reduction)
of 21 to 23 October cut the repository from 101,192 lines to 52,309, 48.3%
fewer: Go fell 29%, Kotlin 39%, and Markdown 75%.

What is wrong with it. It is a
[single-user prototype](https://github.com/NielsdaWheelz/skidbladnir/blob/b0dd12a6d9865dac8938fdbba9d0fae7072d7cc0/docs/architecture.md),
calibrated to one owner, three computers, and one phone. There is no iOS app.
The phone pairs only by scanning a QR code with Google Play services. There is
no retained behavioral test suite. Each change is proven by
[temporary tests](https://github.com/NielsdaWheelz/skidbladnir/blob/b0dd12a6d9865dac8938fdbba9d0fae7072d7cc0/docs/history.md#5-temporary-tests-over-a-retained-suite)
at its boundary, then deleted; CI catches invalid source and broken builds,
not session targeting, input delivery, pairing, terminal interaction, or
client behavior. Sessions die with their tmux server, and I have not yet
observed recovery from a real reboot.

The calibration settles the rest: "Exact targets and at-most-once effects are
not negotiable; availability and polish are."
